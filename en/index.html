The world of Lumeria

Owls, nagas, maned wolves, rhinos, birds of prey and humans shared Lumeria in peace. Then the Consecrated learned to turn people into stained glass, and called it the Blessing.

Every Lumeria Studio game tells a piece of this story, always in carefully crafted pixel art.
